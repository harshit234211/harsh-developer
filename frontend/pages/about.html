01

Discovery & Scope

We dissect your business goals, user journeys, functional constraints, and technical feasibility to produce a fixed timeline and milestone roadmap.

02

Architecture & UI/UX

We design database schemas, REST/WebSocket API contracts, and high-fidelity interactive component wireframes before writing code.
